1 the main idea

the question hides inside

A direct question shouts. It has a question mark. It has the particle mi.

A reported question doesn’t shout. The mark goes. The particle goes. The verb stands as a pair.

Gelecek misin?
He asked whether you’d come.

In English the question hangs on “whether.” Turkish has no “whether.” It has a pair. Yes and no. Side by side.

3 why the pair

remove the “yes.” the meaning breaks.

The pair works like English “whether.” Remove half. See for yourself.

gelmeyeceğini
Gelip gelmeyeceğini sordu. He asked whether you’d come.

Without the pair it’s a statement. He said you won’t come. No question anymore.

4 tense

yesterday or tomorrow

Tense sits in the second half. There are two options. -DIK covers past and present. -AcAK covers the future. Tap a dot.

Gelip gelmeyeceğini bilmiyorum.

I don’t know whether he’ll come.

Yesterday and usually wear the same form. Context decides. English can do that too. I don’t know whether he goes to the gym. In general. Not right now.

5 person

whose coming?

The person clips onto the second half. Like a badge. Tap a color. You’ll hear it.

Trap. “You” and “he” have the same form. Want clarity? Put a word in front. Onun gelip gelmeyeceğini sordu. He asked whether he’d come.

6 case

the boss picks the tail

The last letter isn’t yours. The main verb picks it. Asked what? Sure about what? Decided on what?

the boss wants-ı / -i
Gelip gelmeyeceğini sordu.

He asked whether he’d come.

After a person ending in -i, an n slips in. A cushion. That’s why it’s -ni, -nden, -ne.

7 question word

got ne? no pair needed

Who, where, when, why. These words already ask. They don’t need yes-no.

8 no verb

no verb? borrow olmak

Married? Home? No verb here. Just a word. We borrow the verb from olmak. The pair forms by itself. Flip the cards.

9 lab

build your own sentence

Three modes. Build it yourself. Guess the tail. Recognize it by ear.

Start with building. Turn the parts and watch what changes. Then hide the hints. At the end, only your ears are left. Made a mistake? That’s fine. Nothing happens to you for it here.
