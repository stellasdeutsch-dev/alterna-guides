04
Tool one

The flag: -dan beri(point)

There's a point on the calendar: a year, a month, a day, morning, three in the afternoon. You plant a flag. You say: from this point until now.

ekimdenberi

Flag planted: ekimden beri — since October.

The flag stays put. Time moves on, but the flag remembers where it all began.

You already know the -DAn tail: the ablative. Voiced — dan, den. After voiceless ç f h k p s ş t — tan, ten.

(01)2020'den beri burada çalışıyorum.I've worked here since 2020.
(02)Sabahtan beri yağmur yağıyor.It's been raining since morning.
(03)Pazartesiden beri hastayım.I've been sick since Monday.
(04)Ocaktan beri spor yapıyorum.I've been working out since January.

05
Tool two

The tape measure: -dır(stretch)

There's a number and a unit: three years, two hours, a week. That's a stretch of time. You measure it with the tape. The -DIr tail sticks straight onto the unit. No beri at all.

Drag the slider or the tape itself. Change the unit. Listen to the tail change.

Minute, hour, day, week, month, year. Six units for life.

for three years
(01)Üç yıldır İstanbul'da yaşıyorum.I've lived in Istanbul for three years.

06
Eight tails

-dır, -dir, -dür and friends

The vowel listens to the word's last vowel. The consonant listens to the last sound: after a voiceless one — t. Just like -DAn, only with four vowels.

Learn one exception right away: saat. People say saattir, even though the vowel is a.

Tap a card to hear it.

08
English and Turkish

A different tense

English switches to the present perfect: I've lived here for three years, I've been waiting since morning. Turkish just uses the present: yaşıyorum, bekliyorum.

So drop the have. The suffix already says how long. The verb only says it's still going.

I've lived here for three yearsÜç yıldır burada yaşıyorum.
We've worked here since OctoberEkimden beri çalışıyoruz.
We've been married for five yearsBeş yıldır evliyiz.

Traps

Five potholes on the route(05)

Everyone trips on these. That's normal. What's not normal is waiting three hours for a friend and reporting five minutes.

(01)Üç yıl beriÜç yıldır

A number and a unit — tape measure. Beri doesn't get along with numbers.

(02)Pazartesi beriPazartesiden beri

Beri never stands alone. -DAn always comes before it.

(03)EkimdirEkimden beri

October is a point. You don't measure a point with a tape.

(04)İki saatdırİki saattir

After voiceless t — t again. And saat takes front i.

(05)Zaten üç yıl burada yaşıyorumÜç yıldır burada yaşıyorum

Don't add zaten (“already”). The tail does the job by itself.
