4kinds of meaning glitches

You're in Samsun. Renting an apartment by the sea. You write to the landlord. Nicely, like a grown-up. It's C1, after all.

Yaklaşık on gün kadar kalacağım.I'll stay about ten days or so.

The landlord reads. The landlord is silent. A minute later he writes:

Yaklaşık mı, kadar mı?So is it “about” or “or so”?

You say, what do you mean. He says, well, pick one. Your grammar is perfect. The sentence is broken. Turks call this anlatım bozukluğu.

Guess first. Which word goes?

Pick your glitch

Meaning glitches come in four kinds. Every word is in place, the grammar is clean. And the sentence limps. Tap any.

Glitch 1

Extra word

Some words have already said it all. And you add one more. Just to be safe. Like a belt on top of suspenders.

Two words, one meaning — one is extra.

The test is simple. Strike the word. Meaning still there? Then it was extra.

Glitch 2

Contradiction

You say “definitely.” And right away “maybe.” The other person doesn't know whether to put the kettle on.

A hundred percent and fifty can't live in one sentence.

Confident words: kesinlikle, tam, her zaman, hiç şüphesiz. Doubtful ones: galiba, aşağı yukarı, genellikle and the tail -ebilir. Keep one team.

Glitch 3

Wrong word

The word looks similar. From the same box. But it's not the one.

A phone is expensive. A price is high. Telefon çok pahalı. Fiyatlar çok yüksek.

A thing — pahalı, ucuz. A number — yüksek, düşük.

A trap pair: çekingen is shy. çekimser abstained from a vote. Mix them up and your bashful neighbor suddenly goes to the polls.
