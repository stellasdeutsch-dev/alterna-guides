Freeze-frame. The verb gets -mA and an owner: geri vermen, “your returning.”

Then the word şartıyla, “with the condition.” Literally: with the condition of your returning.

You remember -DIğI takdirde, “in case.” That's a case. This is a requirement.

“You can take the book provided that you return it tomorrow.”

The condition is general or for the same person:

Who meets the condition shows in the owner: vermem, vermen, vermesi and so on.

Is the condition for everyone or for the same person? Then the owner is optional: erken gelmek koşuluyla.

If a third party meets it, you need the owner, in the genitive: Ali'nin ödemesi şartıyla.

Scene 5Knowledge base

Contract or conversation

ŞART · KOŞUL
şartıyla

Contracts, rules. Sounds strict.

koşuluyla

The same thing. A simpler word.

-sA

Geri verirsen alabilirsin.

-DIğI takdirde

Geciktirdiğiniz takdirde ceza var.

şart and koşul are synonyms. It's also written separately: şartı ile.

In conversation, -sA is enough: geri verirsen alabilirsin. Contracts aren't written that way.

“In case of late payment, a fine”: that's takdirde. A case, not a requirement.

After the condition there's usually a permission: alabilirsin, kalabilirsiniz.
