02 Layers

tanıştırıldı

tanış + tır + ıl + dı

with each other

someone set it up

done to us

already happened

I sit down to take the word apart. It's built in layers, like a burger.

tanış — to get acquainted. On your own, with each other. That's the reciprocal -ş.

Plus the causative -tır: someone sets it up. tanıştır — to introduce others.

Plus the passive -ıl: the action is done to you. By whom — off camera.

Plus tense. tanıştırıldı — they were introduced.

The order is ironclad: stem, reciprocal, causative, passive, tense. The passive always comes last before tense.

Next question: which passive goes after the causative?

Usually there are three. After a vowel, -n: okundu. After l — -In: bilindi. Otherwise -Il.

The causative always ends in t or r. So what follows is only -Il.

That leaves harmony: -ıl, -il, -ul, -ül. The choice is one vowel.
