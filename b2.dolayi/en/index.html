01 / three lenses[look / 01]

Three lenses

One fact. Your glasses.

The snow closed the schools. That's a fact. Then you put on glasses. Clear, red, or pink. Switch them.

ankara · kızılaylens 01 / clear
Kar nedeniyle okullar tatil oldu. newscaster · calm

Clear glasses — dolayı and nedeniyle. Just a fact. Red — yüzünden, the cause is to blame. Pink — sayesinde, you're grateful to the cause. Notice? One word changes. The rest of the sentence stays put.

03 / profilebenim · senin · onun

Because of me. Thanks to you.

With pronouns, yüzünden and sayesinde take a possessive tail. Like benim evim, only instead of a house it's a cause.

nedeniyle doesn't get along with pronouns. For “because of this” there's a ready-made bu nedenle.

Memorize the pairs. You — sayende. He — sayesinde. Me — sayemde.

04 / campaign builder[lab]

The lab

Build a campaign

Pick a cause and a frame. The machine will build the sentence and show how it sounds. There are three modes: word, pronoun, whole action.

In the third mode the action first becomes a noun: geç kalmak → geç kalmam. With dolayı, -DIğIndAn is handier: geç kaldığımdan dolayı. You already know this from -DIğI için.

05 / wrong frame[fit check]

Traps

Everyone trips on these. That's normal. Four mistakes — four frames in the wrong size. Hit “Fix.”

01wrong frameperfect fit
Senin yüzünden sınavı geçtim.
Senin sayende sınavı geçtim.

With yüzünden it sounds like a reproach. Your friend will be offended.

02wrong frameperfect fit
Yağmur dolayı maç ertelendi.
Yağmurdan dolayı maç ertelendi.

dolayı doesn't hold without the ablative.

03wrong frameperfect fit
Kardan nedeniyle okullar tatil oldu.
Kar nedeniyle okullar tatil oldu.

-DAn is only for dolayı. Here it's the bare word.

04wrong frameperfect fit
Sen sayesinde başardık.
Senin sayende başardık.

With a pronoun you need a possessive tail: you means senin sayende.

News vs. chat

One snowy Ankara. The newscaster loves nedeniyle and dolayı. Friends in the chat say yüzünden and sayesinde. Switch and tap the messages.

In everyday speech you'll hear dolayı too. But the news can't live without nedeniyle.
