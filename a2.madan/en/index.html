01 Story

The menemen is getting cold

You're in İzmir. You rent a room from Aunt Ayşe. Morning. There's menemen on the table. You're running late.

You say: Kahvaltı yapmadan önce çıkıyorum. Confidently. With an accent, but confidently.

Aunt Ayşe nods. Covers the menemen with a plate. Sits down to wait.

Because you said: “I'm leaving before breakfast”. So you'll be back for breakfast.

Eleven a.m. A message. The menemen is getting cold. Aunt Ayşe isn't.

03 Spot it yourself

Six boxes. What do they share?

None of the actions happened. Look at what grew on the end of the verb.

The verb is whole. Then ma — “not”. Then dan — “from”.

English needs “without” plus an -ing form. Turkish just hangs two bricks on the verb. One says “not”, the other says “from”. Together — “without doing”.

04 Rule

Three bricks and done

We stack it like boxes on a shelf. Hit “next” or turn on autopilot. The last brick is the main action. Without it the sentence hangs in midair.

verbnotfrom
  1. Take the verb stem. No -mak.
  2. Add ma / me — that's “not”.
  3. Add dan / den — the same “from” tail.
  4. Glue it together and add the main action.

question: where's the “not”?

05 Harmony

Just two forms

The tail listens to the verb's last vowel. Two options, like in -dan / -den. Tap a vowel — it'll ride off to its box.

It's the same harmony as in -dan and -den. Nothing new.

06 Who does it

The tail doesn't change

The person lives only in the main verb. -madan is locked in its box and doesn't move. Like English “without eating”: it doesn't change either.

Handy: learn one tail and it fits everyone. You, Aunt Ayşe, all of İzmir.

kahvaltı yapmadan

I'm leaving without having breakfast.

first “without doing”, then the main action

07 The main trap

One önce changes everything

You already know -madan önce — “before”. Without önce you get “without doing”. Turn önce on and off. Watch the timeline.

This is the very mistake from the story. It's why menemen goes cold all over the country.

13 Traps

Where everyone trips

That's normal. Tap the green — the mistake fixes itself. You're allowed to mess up here. No menemen will be harmed.

yapmadan önce çıkıyorum

With önce it's “before”. Want “without doing”? Drop the önce.

yapmamadan

The “not” is already inside. A second ma turns the sentence into mush.

gidmeden

The t only softens before a vowel: gidiyor. Before m it stays t.

yapmadanım çıkıyorum

The person goes only on the main verb. The -madan tail takes no endings.

yemedan

The vowel in ye is e. So it's meden.
