Izmir. The Kordon waterfront. Alina finishes her coffee and gets up.

On her way out she waves to the barista: Güle güle! The barista laughs.

He says, güle güle is what I say to you. You're leaving, you say Hoşça kal.

Alina opens the dictionary. gülmek — to laugh. Güle güle — “laughing-laughing.”

So she wished the barista a nice trip out of his own café. Laughing.

Cut the word: gül + e. The root and a short tail -(y)A.

One of these forms can't survive alone. There are always two: güle güle.

The vowel follows the last one in the root: koş → koşa, gül → güle.

Root ends in a vowel — add y: oku → okuya.

git → gide: t gets voiced before a vowel. No personal endings.
